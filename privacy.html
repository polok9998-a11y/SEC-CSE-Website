Privacy Policy

Legal
Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SEC CSE Batch 2026 student website (“we”, “our”, “this site”) handles information when you visit. We keep it simple and honest: this is a static, student-maintained website, and it does not collect, store or process your personal data on any server.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through this website.

  • There are no user accounts, registrations or forms on this site.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.
  • Browsing this site is anonymous; nothing you do here is sent to us.

2. Local Storage (Theme Preference)

To remember whether you prefer light or dark mode, this site saves a single preference in your browser’s local storage under the key theme. This is:

  • not personal data — it contains only the words “light” or “dark”;
  • stored on your own device and never shared with anyone;
  • removable at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

3. Published Contact Details

The Contact page publishes the names, phone numbers and email addresses of batch representatives, shared voluntarily for academic and batch coordination purposes only. If your details appear on this site and you would like them updated or removed, please email us and we will do so promptly.

4. Third-Party Links

This site links to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Facebook and email clients. Once you leave this site, the privacy policies of those services apply. We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, their content or practices.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email cse19batch@university.edu.
